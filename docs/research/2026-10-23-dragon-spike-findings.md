## Notes that affect the design

- `/damage` and effects do not hurt the dragon, and the console-spawned stationary arrow and snowball entities that were tested caused no damage. Projectiles fired by a player (a real bow draw and release with player-input aiming) were not tested, so that stays unresolved (follow-up spike JDW-207); do not read this as a result for all projectiles. Console-driven nerfing means the pack override (health) or `kill`; console damage-testing in the lab arena cannot use `/damage`.
- Console reach: `send-command` in the image fails when `docker exec` runs as the default user (needs `-u 1000:1000`; process list search hits `/proc/1/exe`). Used a direct write to the server's fd 0.
- `transport=nethernet` is the image default; a RakNet client (gophertunnel) needs `TRANSPORT=raknet` and `ALLOW_LIST=false` with `ONLINE_MODE=false` or BDS refuses to start.
- Endermen spawned in groups of 4 in the End near the bot (10 `AddActor` groups in 60 s); the End spawns hostile mobs the bots will see.
- Perch height depends on world state: 59.9 with no exit portal, 65.2 after a dragon has died there.
- Crystal blast near the dragon is the only damage source verified, and it gave 22 per blast (about 11% of vanilla HP, 40% of a 50 HP dragon) and crystals heal about 2 HP/s.
