Guide

Logging in

  1. Join the server in Minecraft.
  2. Open this page. It shows a short code and the command to type.
  3. In game chat, type !map CODE with the code from the page.
  4. Wait a moment. The page unlocks by itself.

A code lasts ten minutes and works once. To log out of the map everywhere, type !map logout in game chat. This is the way out if you ever typed a code from someone else’s screen. The Log out button in the menu logs out this browser only.

Moving around

Drag to pan and scroll or pinch to zoom. The tabs at the top switch between the Overworld, the Nether and The End. Go to coordinates in the menu takes you to a place by its numbers, and Copy link gives you a link to what you are looking at.

Layers

The Layers panel turns things on and off: players, mobs, markers, structures, biomes, trails and more. Each group opens into its parts. A line can be shown on its own, or its whole group hidden, from the line’s own menu. On a keyboard, the arrow keys, Space and Enter work inside the panel.

Search

The search box finds players, biomes, structures, markers and your own waypoints. It also takes places typed as numbers:

120 -340
Go to X 120, Z -340. 120 64 -340 and 120, -340 work too.
chunk 7 -21
Go to that chunk and pin it.
spawn
The world spawn.
me
Where you are, while you are online.
@name
Players only, by gamertag.

Follow and inspect

Tap a player, mob, marker or structure to open its card. Follow keeps the map centred on it as it moves, Go to jumps there once, and Copy x y z copies its coordinates. Players also have a Details button.

History

Where the server keeps it, History opens a bar under the map. Step back day by day to see how the terrain looked, play the days through, compare two days, or show what changed.

Saved views

Views saves where you are looking and which layers are on, so you can come back to it. They are kept in your browser, and a view can be shared as a link.

Keyboard shortcuts

These work when you are not typing in a box. Esc always works. The map has a Single-key shortcuts on switch, in the list under the ? button, to turn the single keys off.

/
Search the map
Esc
Clear the search, then close the card
G
Grid and chunk focus on or off
L
Layer panel open, or put away
P
Pause or resume live positions
F
Follow, or stop following
1 2 3
Overworld, Nether, The End
V
Saved views
S
Go to the world spawn
M
Go to where you are, while you are online
N
The next group of markers
+ -
Zoom in and out
W
Zoom out to the whole world
B
Zoom in to block detail
D
The debug panel on or off
?
The list of shortcuts
