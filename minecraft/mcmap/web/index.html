    Shortcuts and search tips

    Keys

    /
    Search the map
    Esc
    Clear the search, then close the card; in the search box, a second press leaves it
    G
    Grid and chunk focus on or off
    L
    Layer panel open, or put away to its rail
    P
    Pause or resume live positions
    F
    Follow, or stop following, what the card is about
    1 2 3
    Overworld, Nether, The End
    V
    Saved views; in the list, 1 to 9 switches to that view
    S
    Go to the world spawn
    M
    Go to where I am, while I am online
    + -
    Zoom in and out
    ?
    This list

    Shortcuts do nothing while you are typing in a box. Esc always works.

    In the layer panel

    ↑ ↓
    The line above or below. Home and End go to the first and the last
    → ←
    Open or close what a line holds, or go to the line it is under
    Space
    Show or hide the line
    Enter
    Open or close a group; on a player, a named mob or a waypoint, go to it
    Shift F10
    What else can be done with the line: show only it, show or hide its group, zoom to it
    A letter
    The next line that begins with it

    While the focus is in the list, a letter that begins a line finds that line, and is the shortcut above only if no line begins with it. On the panel’s edge, ← and → resize it, Home and End go to its narrowest and widest, and Enter puts it away.

    In the search box

    120 -340
    Go to X 120, Z -340. 120 64 -340 and 120, -340 work too
    chunk 7 -21
    Go to that chunk and pin it
    spawn

    me
    Where I am, while I am online
    @name
    Players only, by gamertag
