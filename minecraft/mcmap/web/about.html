About the map

This is a web map of the FWB Minecraft Bedrock server. It shows the world from above: the land, the Nether and The End, and the places worth finding.

What you can see

  • The terrain, drawn from a copy of the world. It is refreshed from time to time, so a new build can take a while to show up.
  • For players who are logged in: who is online and where, the mobs around, bases, chests, beds, structures, biomes and the paths players have walked.

The map itself asks you to log in from the game, which shows you play on the server. These pages need no login and show nothing about the world.

Who runs it

It is a private community project, run by the server owner for the people who play on the server. It is not for profit: there is no charge, no advertising and no sale of anything.
