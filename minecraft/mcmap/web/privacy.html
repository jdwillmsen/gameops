Privacy

The map keeps as little about you as it can. This page says what that is. It is a plain description of how the map works, written by the person who runs it.

What the map knows about you

  • Who you are in game. When you log in with !map, the server tells the map your Xbox user ID (XUID) and gamertag. They go into your login cookie, and your XUID is noted in the list of players below. No record of each login is kept. When you ask to be logged out everywhere, the time of that request is kept with your XUID, so your older logins stop working.
  • Where you are. While you are online, your position is shown live to every player who is logged in to the map. Trails are an optional feature: where the operator has switched them on, a trail of where you have been is kept in the map’s memory for as long as the operator sets, up to a week, and older points are thinned out. Trails are never written to disk, and a restart of the map clears them.
  • Your head. Your skin’s face may be used as your marker while you are online. It is kept in memory only and dropped a few minutes after you leave.
  • Your waypoints. Places you save in game with !waypoint are shown on the map to you and nobody else.
  • The world. Terrain and places such as beds and chests are read from the server’s world. Where the operator has switched history on, the map keeps a picture of the terrain and its markers for each day, and does not delete old days automatically.
  • The list of players. The map keeps a list of every player it has seen online: the ID the game gives you, your gamertag and up to five you had before, and when it first and last saw you. Once you log in to the map with !map or Xbox, your XUID is added, so operators can be marked as such. Any logged-in player can see the list, but not your XUID. A full list of 1,024 players makes room by dropping the player seen longest ago.
  • What you have done. Running totals for each player: deaths and kills by kind, blocks broken and placed by kind, joins, leaves and time online, counted from when the map started keeping them. Only the totals are kept, never the events. Any logged-in player can see them in a player’s details.
  • Operators. Server operators are the map’s admins. The map reads who they are from the game and keeps their XUIDs, with when that was last confirmed, so admin still works for up to a day while the game cannot be asked.
  • Passkeys. If you add a passkey, the map keeps its public key and ID, a counter your device updates, your XUID and gamertag, the name you gave it, and when it was added and last used. The private key stays on your device. Removing the passkey, or logging out everywhere, deletes it.
  • Players the map knows. Each time you log in with !map, the map notes your XUID with when it first and last saw you, so that you can sign in with Xbox later without the game. A player last seen more than 180 days ago, or who logs out everywhere, is forgotten.
  • Signing in with Xbox. If the operator has switched it on, you can sign in with your Microsoft account instead of typing a code. Microsoft tells the map your XUID and gamertag; the map checks that you are a player it knows, or are on the server’s allowlist, and then forgets everything Microsoft sent. It keeps no Microsoft password, token or email address.
  • Logs. The map’s logs record that a login was issued, with the XUID and gamertag it was issued to, and when logins are withdrawn. Your XUID can also appear there when something goes wrong with your own data, such as your waypoints.

Cookies

The map sets only cookies needed to log in, none for tracking. All are named with a __Host- prefix, so a browser sends them only to this exact site over a secure connection, and all are HttpOnly, so scripts on the page cannot read them.

  • __Host-mcmap_pending: set while you wait for your login to complete, so only the browser that asked for a code can collect it.
  • __Host-mcmap_passkey: set for a few minutes while you log in with a passkey, so the answer your device gives is matched to the request this browser made.
  • __Host-mcmap_xbox: set for up to ten minutes while you sign in with Xbox, so the answer Microsoft sends back is matched to the request this browser made.
  • __Host-mcmap_session: your login. It holds your XUID and gamertag, when it was issued and when it ends, signed so it cannot be altered. By default it lasts seven days, twelve hours after a passkey login, or a day after signing in with Xbox.

What stays in your browser

Settings such as the theme, the layers you turned on, your saved views and the live-update pace are kept in your browser’s local storage. They are never sent to the server. Clearing your site data removes them.

Who sees what

Only players who have logged in from the game can see the map, including where other players are right now. These pages, and the login screen, show nothing about the world.

No tracking, no third parties

The map has no analytics, no advertising and no social-media widgets. Its pages load everything from the map’s own address, and nothing about you is sent to anyone else.

Logging out and deletion

Type !map logout in game chat to end every login of yours, on every device. The Log out button ends the one in the browser you are using. To have anything else about you removed, email {{contact}} or ask the server operator in game.
